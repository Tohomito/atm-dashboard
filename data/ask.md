# ATM Audience Dashboard: full data file

Built 2026-10-19. Dashboard: https://atm.mattbecher.com. This file holds every figure the dashboard cites, so questions can be answered from all of it.

## How to read this file

- **Daily figures** (LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, site) come from a Google Sheet that the dashboard reads live each time it opens. The question that brought you here includes the latest of those figures, and any posts newer than this file. Prefer those for anything recent.
- **Post history** below runs from May 2026 to 2026-10-05. Newer posts are in the question.
- **Monthly figures** come from the monthly audience scorecard. Each chart below states its own date range and source.
- Name sources by platform (LinkedIn, Instagram, beehiiv, GA4), not by person. Call a baseline by its month ("September baseline").
- "At The Moment" is a separate LinkedIn page with a similar name, watched as a peer. It is not an ATM page; never add its figures to ATM's.
- Say plainly when the data cannot answer a question, and what data would.

## Current analysis

**Conversation is up, reach is down, and partners and collabs did the lifting.**

LinkedIn drew far more comments in September while impressions fell a quarter from August, partly by design: filming slowed in August, so September had fewer new pieces. The posts that travelled were the ones a guest, honoree or partner shared: reposts with their own words took a post to 7–8 times its organic engagement, and Instagram's best posts were all collabs. Instagram reach is fading into October, YouTube is now all organic and adding subscribers faster than the ad flight did, and the newsletter list grew on imports while clicks per delivered email fell.

Window: The read · Sep 1 to Oct 4, 2026

### Findings

- **LinkedIn: fewer impressions, more conversation.** Organic impressions were 54,039 in September, down 25% on August, and reach per post sits 25% below the June–August benchmark (901 against 1,196). Part of the drop was planned: filming slowed in August, so September had fewer new pieces. Comments went the other way: 161 on posts published in the 30 days to Oct 5, against 60 in the 30 days before. LinkedIn's own engagement rate held near 8.7%. (Now; A)
- **Partners carried the reach where they took part.** Reposts with the partner's own words took The Future of Growth Ep2 to 8.2 times its organic engagement and Will McIntosh's Moment Maker to 7.7 times. Across all 38 September posts the lift was only 1.28 times, because most posts had no such repost. Repost tracking began in September, so this is the first month it can be measured. The most-commented posts were people, not announcements: Sara Fischer's honoree poster (60 comments) and Lori Goode's masterclass (37). (C; E; F)
- **Instagram held in September, then faded.** Views were 61,587 for Sep 1–28, up 8.5% on August. By Oct 4, 28-day reach was 28,800, down 33% on the 28 days before and a quarter of July's peak. Likes on posts published in the last 30 days fell from 1,230 to 459 on about the same number of posts (38 against 37). September's three best posts were accepted collabs, which are already standard practice, so the fall is in everything else. (Now; A; E)
- **Follower growth is slowing.** LinkedIn added 3.8% in September against 6.2% a month over the summer, and Instagram is flat at about 8,280. Among peers, ATM ranks sixth on new LinkedIn followers (224 in 30 days), seven behind American Advertising Federation. "At The Moment", a separate page with a similar name, added 83 followers in the same 30 days from a single post, which suggests some follows and tags meant for ATM land there. The LinkedIn bio was rewritten on Sep 30, so October is its first full month. (Now; A; 03)
- **YouTube is organic now, and converts better.** The paid flight ran June to August, so September's fall to 4,421 views (93% below the June–August average) is a spend change, not an editorial one. September added 50 subscribers, more than the 41 that 180,000 paid views produced across June to August. The channel stands at 111 subscribers. (A; B; 04)
- **The newsletter grew on imports; action did not keep pace.** Active subscribers reached 8,407 on Sep 29, up from 5,647 on Sep 1, almost all from the paid list-growth import. That program delivered engaged readers but far fewer than planned. Verified human clicks rose to 236 across seven sends but fell as a share of delivered email, from 0.72% to 0.52%. Site sessions rose 3.4% to 5,739 while users fell 7.2%. (A; 02; 05)

### Priorities

- **Ask every guest and partner to repost with their own words.** It is the strongest lever on the page and most posts go without it. Repost tracking already shows the effect: Michelle Crossan-Matos's own repost drew 200+ likes before anyone at ATM noticed. Send the guest a ready caption and a link on the day the post goes live.
- **On Instagram, work on the posts that are not collabs.** Collabs are already the norm and still win; the reach loss is in the rest. Try the two levers on the Oct 14 backlog, polls in captions and replying to comments in the first few days, and judge them on reach per post.
- **Put LinkedIn's weight behind the formats that already won.** Masterclass questions topped September and now go in every question set; honoree posters drew the most comments; the static news carousel (launched Aug 21) is the top click-through format. Quote posts did not land and were dropped in September; keep them out.
- **Settle whether LinkedIn collab posts work on the page.** The Oct 6 check found no collab option. Until that is settled, partner reach on LinkedIn depends on reposts alone, so ask for one on every post.
- **Judge each channel on the number that shows choice.** YouTube on subscribers, since views fell because the ads stopped. The newsletter on verified clicks, since the list grew by import. The homepage on sign-up rate: the subscribe prompt moved up the page on Oct 1, so compare October's rate with September's, not the totals.

### Stopping mis-tags to "At The Moment"

- **Give every guest and partner the exact tag.** Put "@At The Moment Media (ATM)" and the page link in the ready caption you send them, so nobody picks the similar page from LinkedIn's autocomplete.
- **Check the tag before anything goes live.** Add one line to the go-live checklist: any tag of ATM points to the ATM page, not "At The Moment". Check partner drafts the same way when they share them.
- **Search for mis-tags every week.** Add a weekly step to the daily run: search LinkedIn for recent posts that mention "At The Moment" alongside an ATM guest, series or partner name, and list what it finds. Ask the poster to fix the tag while the post is fresh.
- **Watch the other page's follower jumps.** The daily run already reads the competitor table. Flag any week "At The Moment" gains followers without posting, and line it up with ATM's posts that week: a jump right after a big ATM post points to mis-tags or mis-follows.
- **Log what you find.** Add a "tagged to the wrong page" column to the tagging audit, so the count shows month to month and you can tell whether the fixes work.

### Tracking issues

- **utm_partner does not show in GA4 reports.** It is not a standard GA4 parameter, so it needs a custom dimension before partner data appears. utm_term is standard and unused.
- **Newsletter traffic lands in "Unassigned".** beehiiv tags it newsletter and the episode links use owned_audience; GA4 files both as Unassigned. Set the medium to email, or add a channel group that joins them.
- **Every GA4 event is marked as a key event.** Engaged sessions, bounce rate and conversions mean nothing until the default events are unmarked. The fix only works going forward.
- **The Looker subscriber card is stale.** It has read 5,642 since Aug 31 while beehiiv reports far more. Re-point it, or retire it in favour of beehiiv.
- **No search tracking on the new site search bar.** If it does not send a GA4 event, the searches people run on the site are lost each day.
- **Episode links on social cuts have no UTM of their own.** Without one, traffic from the link layer cannot be told apart in GA4.

### How to read the numbers

- **Followers.** LinkedIn's own total was 3,950 on Oct 4. The daily follower chart tracks organic gains from a fixed starting point, so it reads lower (3,631); use it for the trend, not the total.
- **Engagement rate.** LinkedIn's own rate counts clicks (about 8.7%). The benchmark row in A leaves clicks out (3.25%) so months compare on the same terms.
- **Time windows.** Daily figures run to Oct 4 (LinkedIn reports a day or two late). Monthly figures cover Sep 1–28. Instagram reach and views are Meta's 28-day rolling totals. Engagement momentum counts posts by the date they were published.
- **Subscribers.** beehiiv's active count includes imported addresses, so list size alone overstates readers; verified clicks are the better measure.
- **October will be hard to read.** Several changes landed within ten days: episode links on social cuts (from late September or early October; records differ), the subscribe prompt moved up the homepage (Oct 1), new site features in September, and Awards traffic on top. Partner and content tracking also began in October, so new breakdowns show new visibility, not new performance. Compare rates, not totals.
- **LinkedIn collab posts.** Records disagree on whether the feature is live on the ATM page, and the Oct 6 check found no collab option. Until that is settled, no figure on this page assumes it.
- **"At The Moment".** A separate LinkedIn page with a similar name, not an ATM page. It stays in the peer set and is watched for follows and tags meant for ATM.
